## Overall Impression

The core payment decision on "Mi suscripción" is handled correctly — clear primary CTA, distinguished destructive action — but everything below it collapses into an undifferentiated stack, and the one thing this session was asked to evaluate for multiple personas (the monotributista preference) is the worst-served control in the app: three to four taps deep, hidden inside a billing flow it's conceptually unrelated to, on a full-screen overlay built for a single row, with a toggle that communicates its own state only through which verb happens to be showing. The biggest opportunity is IA, not visual polish: this preference doesn't belong inside "Mi suscripción" at all.

## What's Working

- The primary CTA hierarchy on the core payment decision is correct: filled `bg-emerald-700 h-12` for "Suscribirme con Mercado Pago" clearly outranks the underlined "¿Preferís transferir vos mismo?" — DESIGN.md's button rule respected here.
- Cancel-subscription is visually distinguished (rose outline) from the neutral links, correctly signaling "this is different and consequential" — a good instinct that simply wasn't extended to "Salir."
- Copy stays on-brand rioplatense-informal throughout ("¿Sos monotributista o freelancer?", "avisame acá abajo") — consistent voice.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] What**: "Preferencias de cuenta", the error-message slot, "¿Necesitás ayuda?", and "Salir" all render identically (`text-sm text-slate-500 underline`), with no grouping or icons — four unrelated action classes as one undifferentiated list.
**Why it matters**: Violates recognition-over-recall and consistency; the most common visitor to this screen (someone blocked or managing billing, often stressed) can't distinguish "manage my account" from "get help" from "sign out" without reading every line.
**Fix**: Group by function — cluster the preferences/settings link with an icon, keep support as its own lower-emphasis link, and set "Salir" apart with spacing/an icon since sign-out is a distinct, less-reversible action class.
**Suggested command**: `/impeccable distill`

**[P0] What**: The monotributista toggle exposes no `aria-pressed`/`role="switch"`/`aria-checked` anywhere in its markup (confirmed by direct grep) — state lives only in which verb the button text shows plus a color swap.
**Why it matters**: Screen-reader users get no state announcement at all beyond guessing from the verb; low-vision users get only a subtle text-color change. Compounds DESIGN.md's own already-flagged gap (no visible focus ring on toggles yet).
**Fix**: Add a real switch control (track+thumb) with `role="switch" aria-checked`, and make the label static ("Separación de cuentas") instead of fusing state into the action verb.
**Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] What**: Reaching this one preference costs 3-4 taps (header icon → Mi suscripción → scroll/find the link → Preferencias screen → toggle), with no direct entry point from the dashboard.
**Why it matters**: Directly at odds with the product's own "fricción mínima" principle in spirit — a feature the team invested multiple commits building is now the hardest control to reach in the whole app, nested inside a billing flow it has no logical relationship to.
**Fix**: Surface a direct entry point (e.g. a settings icon in the header) so Preferencias is not a child of Mi suscripción.
**Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout`

**[P1] What**: Toggling the preference ON navigates away and shows a coachmark; toggling OFF shows a toast and leaves the user wherever `mostrarPreferencias`/`mostrarPago` happen to land, with no return to Preferencias.
**Why it matters**: The same control produces two structurally different post-action experiences depending on direction — breaks the mental model and system-status visibility; someone toggling off to "undo" gets no confirmation they're back where they started.
**Fix**: Make both directions land consistently — either both return to Preferencias with a toast, or both navigate with feedback.
**Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

**[P2] What**: Preferencias is a full `fixed inset-0` overlay with its own header and back button, built to hold exactly one toggle row.
**Why it matters**: Disproportionate chrome-to-content ratio; doesn't obviously scale if a second preference is added later, and reads as an empty placeholder screen today.
**Fix**: Design the container to anticipate more rows, or fold it into an existing account/settings surface instead of a dedicated full-screen overlay for one item.
**Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout`

**[P2] What**: Vertical spacing on "Mi suscripción" mixes `mb-2`/`mb-3`/`mb-4` down the card with no visible rule, and the three footer links have no explicit tap-target height (well under 44px, ~12px gaps between them).
**Why it matters**: Ad hoc spacing reads as unpolished; undersized/under-spaced tap targets raise mis-tap risk on what is explicitly a mobile-first app.
**Fix**: Standardize on a spacing scale for this card, and give each footer link real padding/min-height so it's a proper 44px tap target with adequate gaps.
**Suggested command**: `/impeccable audit`

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (impatient power user)** — Wants to flip the toggle and get back to work. Hits the full 3-4 tap chain with zero shortcut, and if he toggles OFF, lands somewhere unpredictable instead of back at his transaction entry flow — costing him extra navigation to recover exactly when he wanted speed.

**Jordan (confused first-timer)** — Opens "Mi suscripción" expecting billing info, finds "Preferencias de cuenta" as one of four identical grey underlined links with no indication it's unrelated to payment — the shared styling implies shared context. On Preferencias, can't tell at a glance whether the toggle is on or off without carefully reading "Activar" vs "Desactivar" — no switch graphic to confirm.

**Sam (accessibility-dependent, screen reader + keyboard-only)** — Tabs through four visually-identical underlined links relying entirely on the announced text for differentiation. The toggle is the real failure: screen reader announces "button, Activar separación de cuentas" with no `aria-pressed` — Sam cannot know whether activating IS the current state or the action about to happen without triggering it first. No visible focus ring on this toggle (a gap DESIGN.md itself already flags) means Sam also can't visually confirm focus before pressing Enter.

**Mica (monotributista freelancer, wants speed, zero patience for money-categorization ambiguity)** — The worst-served persona. Her one goal is reaching this control fast, and instead she's routed through subscription status, possibly an expanded transfer-alias flow, and a "Cancelar suscripción" button she doesn't care about — before she even sees the "Preferencias de cuenta" link at the bottom. Once there, the fused label/state ("Activar…" vs "Desactivar…") is exactly the kind of ambiguity she has no patience for — she has to stop and parse which state she's in before acting, when her goal was a fast, confident flip.

## Minor Observations

- The briefcase icon inside Preferencias is the only icon used across either screen — none of the four Mi suscripción footer links get one, despite icons clearly being part of the app's vocabulary already.
- "Copiar alias" gets `mb-6` while adjacent elements get `mb-3`/`mb-4` — one more sign of ad hoc rather than scaled spacing.
- The screen's own `h1` text changes across three different states (blocked/trial-ended, active-sub, no-sub) — worth confirming all three render at consistent visual weight so the header doesn't reflow awkwardly between them.
