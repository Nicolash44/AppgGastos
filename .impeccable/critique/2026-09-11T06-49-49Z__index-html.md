## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment**: This clears the "could be any expense tracker" bar. The navy ticket-shaped logo, the emerald/rose duo used with zero exceptions across balance/chips/buttons, Space Grotesk reserved strictly for money, the sticky balance header as the one persistent chrome element, and the rioplatense copy ("Papelitos sueltos", "Ya transferí") all reinforce the product's own "Recibo Prolijo" north star. The landing page's device-frame preview shows the *real* UI, not a stock illustration — a specific, confident choice. Where it slips toward generic SaaS: the auth screens (login/registro/recuperar) are competent but interchangeable with any Tailwind SaaS starter.

**Deterministic scan**: The detector found 26 findings — 23 low-contrast pairs, 1 advisory font-size (a hidden loader fallback element, low priority), and 1 "overused font" flag on Space Grotesk. That last one is a **false positive**: both `PRODUCT.md` and `CLAUDE.md` document Space Grotesk for money/titles as an intentional, pinned brand choice, not an accidental default — confirmed independently by both assessments. The 23 low-contrast pairs are real and undocumented: `PRODUCT.md`'s own "Pendiente" section lists missing `aria-label`s and no focus ring as known accessibility gaps, but never mentions contrast — so this isn't an accepted tradeoff, it's an open gap. The most frequent single pair (8 occurrences) is white text on the emerald `#059669` CTA buttons at 3.8:1, short of the 4.5:1 normal-text threshold.

**Visual overlays**: No browser-injectable overlay is available for a static production URL without a local dev server (confirmed by both assessments' setup). In its place, both assessments captured direct screenshots of the live, logged-in app (desktop + mobile) via Playwright — those observations are folded into the findings below.

## Overall Impression

The core financial-facing surface (balance header, delete-confirm copy, semantic color system) is genuinely well-crafted and specific to this product — better than the auth flow around it. The biggest opportunity is structural, not cosmetic: the category chip list has no cap or ordering and already breaks the "≤4 items in a decision point" guideline with only 12 chips in a near-empty test account, directly working against the product's own stated principle of minimal friction to log a movement.

## What's Working

- **The balance header** — sticky, Space Grotesk, sign-colored — is the single best-executed element in the app and reads that way live, not just on paper.
- **Delete-confirm modal copy** — "Vas a borrar Hola de $16.000,00... Tu balance de este mes va a bajar $16.000,00 cuando lo saques" does the math for the user instead of making them infer the consequence. Rare, well-considered microcopy.
- **The landing page's device-frame preview** shows the actual product UI in a navy phone mockup rather than a generic illustration — sells the real thing.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] Category chip row has no cap, grouping, or ordering**
- **Why it matters**: A near-empty test account already renders 12 flat chips (Mercado, Nafta, Mantenimiento Auto, Servicios, Vivienda, Salud, Seguro, Tarjeta de Crédito, Ocio, Ropa, Otros, plus user-added ones) with no sub-grouping or frequency ordering — confirmed visually in both assessments' screenshots. This breaks the working-memory guideline (≤4 items at a decision point) and directly contradicts `PRODUCT.md`'s top stated principle: "fricción mínima al cargar un movimiento." It will only get denser as real users add their own categories.
- **Fix**: Cap visible chips to the most-used N with a "más" expand, or order by frequency/recency instead of insertion order, once the count passes a threshold.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout`

**[P2] Systemic low-contrast text, undocumented as intentional**
- **Why it matters**: 23 detector hits, the largest single group being white text on the emerald CTA button (`#ffffff` on `#059669`, 3.8:1, 8 occurrences) — short of WCAG AA's 4.5:1 for normal text. Secondary/muted text (`#94a3b8` on white/slate-50, 6 occurrences) matches the "Volver"/"Salir" ghost links both assessments independently flagged as visually faint. `PRODUCT.md`'s own accessibility section lists other known gaps (aria-labels, focus rings) but never lists contrast as an accepted tradeoff — this is an open gap, not a documented choice.
- **Fix**: Darken the emerald CTA fill slightly (or bump button text weight/size into the "large text" 3:1 bracket deliberately) and re-check the slate-400 secondary-text family against its usual backgrounds.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable audit`

**[P2] "+Gasto" (expense) toggle defaults active regardless of the account's real transaction mix**
- **Why it matters**: The test account's only real movement is an income entry, yet the dashboard opens on the rose "+Gasto" side by default. For `PRODUCT.md`'s explicitly-named second audience (freelancers/monotributistas tracking variable income), this is a first-paint mismatch — the tool visually assumes "you're here to log spending" before it knows anything about the user.
- **Fix**: Default `tipo` to whichever type has more movements this month (or simply default to `'ingreso'`), instead of hardcoding `'gasto'`.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify`

**[P3] No way to edit a saved movement, only delete-and-recreate**
- **Why it matters**: Docks User Control and Freedom. A typo in an amount or category means deleting a transaction (even with the new confirm step) and re-entering it from scratch, rather than a quick inline edit.
- **Fix**: Add an edit affordance to the movement row (tap to reopen the same form pre-filled), reusing the existing guardar()/categoria selection flow.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

**[P3] Evolución chart has no friendly empty/sparse-data state**
- **Why it matters**: The pie chart ("Gastos por categoría") already got a "Sin gastos este mes" empty state earlier this session — but the Evolución line/bar chart still just renders a flat zero line for new or low-activity accounts, right under the confident balance number, at exactly the moment (the 5-day trial) `PRODUCT.md` says the month-to-month comparison needs to prove its value.
- **Fix**: A short empty-state caption under the chart for all-zero ranges, consistent with the warm tone already used elsewhere ("Cargá tu primer gasto para ver esto tomar forma").
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable onboard`

## A note on an unconfirmed finding

Assessment A's live pass reported the desktop help ("?") popover not visibly opening on click, and a category-limit tooltip appearing unprompted on mobile. I want to flag this rather than bury it: earlier in this same working session, I personally built and Playwright-verified all 5 help icons (open on click/hover, close on the X and on outside-tap) on both an iPhone and a Pixel viewport, live, with zero console errors — so I read Assessment A's result as a likely test-script targeting issue (its own follow-up note says its "?" selector first matched the wrong element) rather than a real regression. Worth a quick manual tap-through on a real phone to be sure, but I'm not carrying it as a headline Priority Issue given the direct contrary evidence I already have.

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (First-Timer)**: Lands on a rose "+Gasto" toggle despite the account's own history being 100% income — the very first interactive impression contradicts the user's own data. The 12-chip category wall (some pre-seeded, some user-added, with no visual distinction) is a lot to parse for someone promised "carga en segundos."

**Sam (Accessibility)**: The white-on-emerald button text and the muted "Volver"/"Salir"/"¿Preferís transferir vos mismo?" links are all below 4.5:1 — confirmed both by the detector and by direct visual read of the "Mi suscripción" screenshot, where those links look faint against the white card. Combined with `PRODUCT.md`'s own admitted gaps (inconsistent aria-labels, no visible focus ring on some chips), this is a real, still-open accessibility debt.

**Casey (Mobile)**: The dashboard stacks balance → toggle → capture form → comparativo → two charts → movement list in one long single column. The core "log a movement" action sits above the fold, which is good, but a rushed one-handed user still scrolls past a lot of secondary content (comparativo, charts) that isn't needed for that specific task.

## Minor Observations

- The Google OAuth "G" icon SVG is duplicated verbatim across login/registro — maintenance debt, not a design flaw.
- The plan price ($16.000) appears as literal text in multiple places per the project's own documentation — a design-system violation waiting to cause drift when the price changes.
- The "Guardar" button renders in a washed-out pink when disabled (empty amount) — plausibly correct disabled-state styling, but worth a quick visual confirmation that it reads as "disabled" and not "broken" at a glance.
- Ghost "‹ Volver" buttons have no visible button affordance (no background/border) — likely fine given the DESIGN.md pattern, but combine that with the low-contrast finding above and it's easy to miss entirely.

## Questions to Consider

- What if the category chip list auto-collapsed to the most-used categories this month plus an explicit "Ver todas" — turning the worst chunking violation into a showcase of "la comparación es el producto"?
- What if the empty/sparse chart states did double duty as onboarding (a ghost/sample overlay: "así se va a ver con tus datos") instead of a bare zero-line, turning a trust valley into reassurance during exactly the 5-day trial window that matters commercially?
- What if `tipo` defaulted per-user instead of hardcoded to `'gasto'` — a small change that would make the tool feel like it's looking at *your* money, not a generic template's assumption?
